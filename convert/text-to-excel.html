How to convert Text to Excel

  1. Drop your Text file in the centre of the wheel, or tap it to browse.
  2. Tap Excel on the wheel. On desktop you can also drag the file while holding Shift and drop it on the Excel segment.
  3. Wait for the browser conversion to finish, then download your Excel file.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Text to Excel converter free?

Yes — free, with no sign-up.

Will my file be uploaded to a server?

No. For documents, images and spreadsheets the conversion runs in your browser. Video runs with ffmpeg.wasm in your browser as well.

Will the output be exactly like the original?

Text and reading order are preserved for text-based files. Complex layouts, embedded fonts and scanned files may need checking — we do not promise a pixel-perfect copy for those.
